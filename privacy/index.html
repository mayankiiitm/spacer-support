Privacy policy

Spacer for iPhone. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Spacer is an iPhone app that makes smaller copies of your videos. This page says what it does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send it yourself.

What Spacer can see

Spacer asks for access to your photo library. It uses that access to:

  • list your videos and read their size, length and format,
  • read a video in order to make a smaller copy of it,
  • save the copy to your library,
  • delete an original, only after you ask it to and confirm in the iOS prompt.

What Spacer sends anywhere

Nothing. Spacer has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. It makes no network connections. Your videos are read and written on your iPhone only.

If a video is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it so Spacer can read it. That download is between your iPhone and your own iCloud account.

Diagnostics

Spacer keeps a small text file on your iPhone with one line for each video it has worked on: the video's format, size and length, how long each step took, and how it ended. It also notes your iPhone model, iOS version and the app's version.

The file holds no video, no file names, no dates of your videos and no locations.

It stays on your iPhone. It is sent only if you open Settings in Spacer, tap Share Diagnostics, and choose where to send it. If you send it to the developer, it is used only to find and fix problems in the app.

Deleting the app deletes the file.

Children

Spacer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
