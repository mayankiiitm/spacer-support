Spacer

Help and support

Spacer is an iPhone app that makes smaller copies of your videos, checks each copy against its original, and lets you decide what to delete. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Common questions

Why isn't one of my videos offered?

Usually because it is already small. Videos saved from chat apps and social apps, and many videos recorded at normal camera settings, have nothing left to remove. Spacer lists those under Already Small and leaves them alone.

Some kinds of video are skipped for now because a smaller copy would lose something: slo-mo, Cinematic, spatial, time-lapse, ProRes, and videos edited in Photos.

How much space will I get back?

It depends on how your iPhone recorded the video. The biggest savings are on videos shot at high quality settings, like 4K at 60 fps on recent Pro models, which often come out at less than half the size. Videos shot at standard settings save less, and some don't shrink at all.

The number in the list is an estimate. Spacer tests a few seconds of a video before spending time on it.

Spacer said a video wasn't worth it. What happened?

Spacer tried it and the copy would not have been meaningfully smaller, so it kept the original and made nothing. That is normal for videos that are already efficient.

Will I see a difference in the copy?

At the default setting the copy is hard to tell from the original unless you zoom in. The slider goes both ways: towards Sharper for copies with no visible difference, towards Smaller for the smallest files.

Resolution, frame rate, colour and HDR stay the same at every setting, and sound is copied untouched. You can compare a copy with its original side by side before you delete anything.

Does Spacer delete my videos?

Never on its own. After a copy is made and checked, you review it and choose to delete the original, and iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted originals stay in Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days.

It stopped part-way through.

Compression runs while Spacer is open. If you switch apps or lock the phone, the video being worked on goes back to waiting and starts again when you come back. Nothing is lost and no half-made copy is saved.

A video shows "stopped unexpectedly on this one".

The app closed while working on that video, so Spacer skips it to avoid doing the same thing again. It will be tried again after the next app update.

Does anything leave my iPhone?

No. There is no account, no upload, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Report a problem

Email spacerdevapp@gmail.com and say what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you have.

If the app stopped or refused a video, Settings in Spacer has Share Diagnostics. It makes a small text file about the videos Spacer worked on, with no video, no file names and no locations in it. Sending it along helps.
